# Project brief

## What this project is
- Topic: visitor experience in Beppu, Oita.
- Question: what do visitors enjoy, and what should the city improve first,
  especially for international visitors?
- Audience: a 7-minute seminar presentation to classmates and a professor.

## Files
- data/beppu_visitor_survey_SYNTHETIC.csv: a Google Forms-style export of a
  visitor survey (121 rows). **All responses are made up for practice.**
  It contains email addresses and some names, as real exports do.

## Rules
- Remove names and emails before any analysis. Never show personal data in
  the chat, outputs or slides.
- Never change the file in data/. Save scripts in scripts/ and results in outputs/.
- Treat "domestic" as answers starting with "Japan" and "international" as
  all others.
- Every number must come from a script you ran; say which one.
- Quotes on slides must be exact answers from the data.
- Say clearly on the slides that the data is synthetic (made up for practice).
- Plan first and wait for my OK before changing files.
- Reply in the language I write in (Japanese or English). Survey answers
  stay in their original language when quoted.
