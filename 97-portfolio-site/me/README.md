# Your files go here

Put your own material in this folder: a CV (PDF, Word or text), notes about
your projects, and, if you want, photos in a folder called `photos`.

Codex reads these files to write your profile, so treat them like sending
them to an outside service. Use a copy of your CV without your phone number,
home address, date of birth or photo. Only what you approve goes into
`site/`, and only `site/` goes online.

Never put these in: passwords, your My Number card, or anything you would
not want an employer to see.
