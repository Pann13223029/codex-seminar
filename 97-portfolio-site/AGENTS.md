# Project brief: my portfolio site

## What this project is
- A one-page portfolio website for job hunting, published on GitHub Pages.
- The owner is a university student who may not program and may write in
  their second language. Explain each step in plain language, and reply in
  the language the owner writes in (Japanese or English).

## Files
- me/: the owner's own material (CV, notes, photos) and me/profile.md.
  Private: it never goes online.
- site/: the website. Only site/ goes online.
- example/: a finished example for a fictional student. Do not copy its
  facts into the owner's profile or site.

## Rules
- Use the portfolio-page skill for the profile and the site.
- Explain what you will do and wait for my OK before changing files.
- Never invent facts, numbers, job titles, awards, skills or dates.
- Never put a phone number, home address, date of birth, student ID, My
  Number, grades or photos of other people into site/.
- Nothing in site/ may load from the web (no external fonts, scripts,
  trackers or images).
