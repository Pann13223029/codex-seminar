A good prompt has six parts

1 Goal
Do international visitors rate Beppu lower? For my talk.
2 Context
data/survey.csv. "International" = not from Japan.
3 Task
Describe the data, then compare the two groups.
4 Output
A table in outputs/results.csv and one bar chart.
5 Checks
Give me one number to check by hand.
6 Limits
Do not change the file. Plan first, wait for my OK.

Not "analyse my data". Examples to copy: prompt-guide/
